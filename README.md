# 📊 Amazon E-Commerce Operations Analysis

### 🔍 Business Problem

An e-commerce business is experiencing inefficiencies in order fulfillment and uneven regional performance, leading to potential revenue leakage and missed growth opportunities. There is limited visibility into which categories, regions, and operational factors are driving performance gaps.

---

### 🎯 Objective

* Analyze regional and category-level performance
* Identify operational inefficiencies in order fulfillment
* Detect revenue concentration and underperforming segments
* Provide actionable, data-driven business recommendations

---

### 📁 Dataset

* 65,000+ order records
* Includes: order ID, category, sales amount, quantity, region, and order status
* Total revenue analyzed: ~$11.89M

---

### 🛠 Tools Used

* SQL (Data extraction & transformation)
* Power BI (Dashboard & visualization)
* Excel (Data preparation)

---

### 🔄 Approach

* Conducted **requirement analysis** to define key business questions and KPIs
* Performed **data cleaning and transformation using SQL**
* Mapped **as-is operational performance** across regions
* Conducted **gap analysis** to identify underperforming areas
* Built an interactive dashboard to track KPIs and performance metrics

---

### 📈 Key Insights

* Revenue is highly concentrated in a few product categories
* Certain regions consistently underperform in order fulfillment
* Imbalanced product distribution leads to missed revenue opportunities
* Operational inefficiencies impact overall performance and scalability

---

### 💡 Recommendations

* Optimize inventory allocation across high-demand regions
* Improve supply chain efficiency for underperforming areas
* Monitor regional KPIs for faster operational decision-making
* Focus on scaling high-performing categories

---

### 🧾 SQL Analysis

Performed data extraction, aggregation, and transformation using SQL to support dashboard creation and business analysis.

---

### 🧠 Business Impact

Enabled identification of regional and operational gaps, supporting data-driven decisions to improve efficiency and revenue performance.
